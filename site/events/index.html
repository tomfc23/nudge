What lands on the phone

Five kinds, one topic.

Every notification arrives on one private topic. Its kind appears in the title, emoji tag, and priority.

Notification types, triggers, and examples
KindWhen it firesExample
❓ QuestionThe agent asks something or ends a turn asking for input. Forms count too.Pick environment · staging | prod
🔒 PermissionThe agent waits for your approval.read · config.json
✅ FinishedA turn completes without a question.Done in 1m 52s · 9 tools
🚨 ErrorA session or tool call fails. Repeats collapse for 60 seconds.shell failed · ECONNRESET
📣 CustomThe agent calls the ntfy_notify tool.Whatever the agent wrote

Question and permission alerts are urgent; errors are high priority; finished alerts use the default priority.
